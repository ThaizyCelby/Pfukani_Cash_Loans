What to include in your message

To get a faster, more accurate response, include the following in your first message:

1

Your full name: as it appears on your SA ID.

2

The amount you're considering: for example, "R3,000".

3

The term you prefer: 1, 2, or 3 months. If unsure, say so and we'll advise.

4

Your employment status: employed, self-employed, grant recipient, or other.

5

Any questions you have: ask them all at once. There's no limit.

Do not send documents in your first message. Wait for us to confirm the correct channel and identity of the person you're speaking to. This protects you from impostors.

Quick answers

Can I apply over the phone?

No, we don't take loan applications by phone. Please use WhatsApp or email this creates a written record and lets you review everything before committing. If you prefer, we can arrange an in-person appointment.

Do you have other offices or agents?

No. Pfukani Cash Loans operates from a single office at Stand No. 601, Maphata Village, Giyani. Any person claiming to represent us from another location is not authorised. Always confirm before sharing documents.

How do I know a message is really from Pfukani?

Our only WhatsApp number is 063 036 0726. We never ask you for your PIN, OTP, or online banking password. If in doubt, phone that number directly before responding.

What if I need to speak to a specific person?

Ask for them by name in your message. If that person is unavailable, another consultant will respond and pass your message on.
