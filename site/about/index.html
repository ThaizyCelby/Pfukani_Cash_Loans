Our story

Who we are

The name Pfukani means "wake up" or "uplift" a fitting name for a business whose purpose is to help people move forward when short-term cash flow gets in the way of everyday life.

We operate from our office at Stand No. 601, Maphata Village, Giyani, and serve customers across Limpopo with short-term financial options from R200 to R10,000 over 1, 2, or 3 months.

We are a registered credit provider under the National Credit Act (NCRCP17495), and every loan we issue follows the same principle: clear terms, transparent costs, and a repayment plan you can realistically afford.

We're not a bank and we don't pretend to be. We're a small, local lender focused on one thing — helping our neighbours get through a tight month without creating a bigger problem next month.

Purpose

Why we exist

Our mission

To provide short-term credit that is genuinely affordable, transparently priced, and responsible so that a temporary cash-flow problem never becomes a long-term financial burden.

Our vision

To be the most trusted micro-lender in Limpopo known not for aggressive marketing, but for honest pricing, fair treatment of every customer, and full compliance with the National Credit Act.

Common questions

About the business

Are you a registered credit provider?

Yes. Pfukani Cash Loans is registered with the National Credit Regulator under NCRCP17495, and we operate in full compliance with the National Credit Act 34 of 2005.

Are you part of a larger group or franchise?

No. Pfukani is an independent, locally owned business based in Giyani, Limpopo. We operate from a single office.

Where can I check that you are who you say you are?

You can verify our NCR registration at ncr.org.za using the number NCRCP17495, or contact the NCR directly on 0860 627 627. We encourage every customer to do this with any lender.

Do you have other branches?

No. We operate from a single office at Stand No. 601, Maphata Village, Giyani. Any person claiming to represent Pfukani elsewhere is not authorised.
