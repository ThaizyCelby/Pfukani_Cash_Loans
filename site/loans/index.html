  • •Interest, capped in terms of the NCA formula
  • •A once-off initiation fee, within the NCA limit
  • •A monthly service fee, within the NCA limit
  • •Credit insurance, only if you choose to take it

What we never charge

  • •An "application fee" before you are approved
  • •A "document processing" or "admin" fee
  • •Any penalty for settling early (NCA §125)
  • •Interest or fees above the NCA caps ever

Statutory Rate Disclosure

Interest rates and applicable fees will be disclosed in your personalised quotation before you enter into a credit agreement. Pfukani Cash Loans does not charge unapproved upfront processing fees. All terms are subject to formal verification and credit review, and no loan will be granted without a completed affordability assessment in terms of the National Credit Act.

Quick answers

Can I repay my loan early?

Yes. Under section 125 of the NCA you may settle your loan in full at any time, and interest is recalculated to the settlement date. No penalty is charged for early settlement.

How much can I borrow on my first loan?

First-time borrowers typically start between R200 and R3,000. Amounts increase as we see consistent repayments. The maximum is R10,000.

Do you require collateral?

No. We never ask you to surrender your ID, bank card, or any document as security. Every loan is unsecured and based purely on affordability.

How fast is disbursement?

Once your documents are verified and your agreement is signed, funds are transferred via EFT — typically the same business day or the next working day.
