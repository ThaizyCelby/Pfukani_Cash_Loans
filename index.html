About Pfukani

Responsible credit solutions in Giyani, Limpopo built on clarity, compliance and respect for the consumer.

Pfukani Cash Loans is an authorised South African credit provider registered with the National Credit Regulator under NCRCP17495. From Stand No. 601, Maphata Village, Giyani, we serve customers with short-term financial options from R200 to R10,000.
